Strategic Positioning
“We don't just manage EV chargers. We help charging station owners manage their entire charging business.”

The product is an EV charging business operating platform. It connects physical charging machines, station owners and EV drivers. The company does not need to manufacture charging hardware; it integrates compatible existing chargers via standard OCPP protocols and provides the software layer used to operate the station, manage workforce & tariffs, and serve drivers.

Station Operations, Incident Response, Workforce & Unit Economics
Section 5.1 & 5.2

Station Operations & Incident Workflow

Multi-station live monitoring with distinct states: Online, Charging, Idle, Offline, and Fault. A fault alert creates an actionable incident rather than a passive alert.

Actionable Incident Notification High Severity

“Kukatpally Charging Station — Charger B is offline. Fault: communication/hardware fault. Time: 10:42 AM.”

Recommended Action: Assign technician or station staff
Automated Notification Rules (Table 5):
  • Charger Offline: Identify site, charger ID, fault code & severity instantly.
  • Connectivity Loss: Distinguish network dropping from confirmed hardware faults.
  • Staff Absent/Late: Alert station manager against assigned roster and shift rules.
  • Abnormal Session: Flag long or stuck sessions for operator verification.
  • Revenue Anomaly: Detect unexpected drops in daily GMV or energy consumption.
Section 5.3

Employee Attendance & Payroll Engine

Charging stations rely on on-ground attendants for connector handling, vehicle queuing and cleaning. ChargeCore integrates attendance directly into the station's operational ledger.

Friction Points to Eliminate

  • × Forced Account Creation: Mandatory passwords and OTP hurdles while standing at charger.
  • × Opaque Pricing: Hidden platform fees, unexpected idle parking charges or tax surprises.
  • × Phantom Chargers: Listed chargers that are broken, offline or blocked by IC engines.
  • × Payment Failures: Failed wallets with no instant retry or alternate gateway.

ChargeCore Product Remedy

  • ✓ Guest QR Flow: Direct camera QR scan to launch browser session; phone number only for receipt.
  • ✓ Upfront Tariff Breakdown: Exact base rate/kWh, taxes and zero hidden markups shown upfront.
  • ✓ Live Telemetry Heartbeat: Stale-data indicator if charger has not reported within 60 seconds.
  • ✓ UPI Auto-Pay & Instant Fallback: Dual payment routing ensures high session completion rate.
